About My Business

At Chelimo’s Piggery Farm, we are committed to raising healthy, well-nurtured pigs through sustainable farming practices that prioritize animal welfare, community nourishment, and consistent excellence. Our mission is to provide high-quality pork products that not only meet but exceed customer expectations, while contributing to food security and economic growth. With a blend of modern techniques and traditional care, we ensure that every step of our farming process reflects integrity, passion, and trust.

Farm Origin

Chelimo Piggery Farm began as a small family venture in the heart of our village. What started with just two pigs and a dream has grown into a thriving community farm. Our founder, Chelimo, saw how pig farming could transform livelihoods providing food security, income, and unity among neighbors. With help from local farmers and elders, the farm expanded, blending traditional wisdom with modern care practices.Today, we raise healthy pigs in clean pens, use sustainable feeding methods, and share our success with the village through training and employment. Every pig we nurture tells a story of resilience, community, and hope.

We believe farming is more than just business; it’s a commitment to feeding families, supporting local markets, and contributing to food security. By combining traditional farming practices with modern techniques, we produce pigs that are not only healthy but also raised with respect for animal welfare.Whether you’re a wholesaler, retailer, or individual customer, we pride ourselves on offering reliable service and premium products. Our goal is simple: to grow a farm that sustains livelihoods, strengthens communities, and provides nutritious pork for generations to come.

Our farm is more than pig pens and feeding troughs, it is a living heartbeat of the village. Each day begins with watering the pigs under the warm African sun, feeding them with care, and ensuring their pens remain clean and welcoming. Around the farm, laughter of children, wisdom of elders, and the dedication of farmers blend into one community spirit. This photo captures not just the animals we raise, but the people, traditions, and hope that make our piggery a true home for both livestock and village life.

Mission

Our mission is simple yet ambitious: to redefine pig farming through innovation, sustainability, and excellence. We believe farming is more than raising livestock — it’s about creating value, building trust, and driving growth. By combining modern techniques with a passion for quality, we deliver pork products that meet the highest standards while empowering local communities and strengthening the agricultural economy.

Future Prospects

The future of the Farm is bold and exciting. We envision scaling our operations into a fully integrated agribusiness, leveraging technology to enhance efficiency, animal health, and product quality. Our roadmap includes expanding into value-added pork products, exploring export opportunities, and establishing strong partnerships with retailers and restaurants. With sustainability at the core, we aim to become a regional leader in livestock farming — a brand synonymous with innovation, reliability, and impact. Chelimo’s Piggery Farm is not just growing pigs; we’re growing possibilities.
