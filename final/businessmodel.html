My Business Model canvas

Running a piggery farm can be profitable, but it comes with challenges that require proactive management. Common issues include disease outbreaks, feed costs, waste management, and market fluctuations. By anticipating these problems and planning solutions, the farm can remain sustainable and competitive.Below are some of the steps taken;

  1. Vaccinating pigs regulary and quarantine new stock before mixing
  2. Manage Feed Costs.Feed is the largest expense in pig farming, so cost control is vital through the use of locally availiable feed ingredients,balanced nutrition and alternative feeds like maize brand or cassava
  3. Handle Waste Properly.Through Installing proper drainage and manure pits,Converting waste into biogas or organic fertilizers and complying with local environmental regulations
  4. Diversifying the market through adding value to our produce for example sausages
